# Reported speech (mowa zależna): ściąga

Relacjonujemy czyjeś słowa. Gdy czasownik relacjonujący jest w czasie przeszłym (*said, told, asked*), cofamy czas
o jeden krok (**backshift**), zmieniamy zaimki i okoliczniki czasu. *That* po *said / told* można pominąć.

## 1. Zmiany czasów i czasowników modalnych

| Mowa niezależna | Mowa zależna | Przykład |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | Past Simple | 'I **live** in Gdańsk.' → She said she **lived** in Gdańsk. |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous | 'We **are playing** chess.' → They said they **were playing** chess. |
| Past Simple | Past Perfect | 'I **missed** the bus.' → He said he **had missed** the bus. |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect | 'I **have finished**.' → She said she **had finished**. |
| Past Perfect | Past Perfect (bez zmiany) | 'I **had left**.' → He said he **had left**. |
| will | would | 'I **will** help.' → She said she **would** help. |
| can | could | 'I **can** swim.' → He said he **could** swim. |
| may | might | 'It **may** rain.' → She said it **might** rain. |
| must | had to | 'You **must** rest.' → The doctor said I **had to** rest. |
| am / is / are going to | was / were going to | 'I'**m going to** win.' → He said he **was going to** win. |
| would, could, might, should, ought to | bez zmiany | 'You **should** go.' → She said I **should** go. |

**Bez backshiftu:** czasownik relacjonujący w czasie teraźniejszym (*She says she is busy.*) oraz prawdy ogólne i fakty
nadal prawdziwe (*He said the Earth goes / went round the Sun.*).

## 2. Zaimki, określniki, okoliczniki czasu i miejsca

| Mowa niezależna | Mowa zależna |
|---|---|
| I / we | he, she / they |
| my / our | his, her / their |
| you | I, me, we (zależnie od tego, do kogo mówiono) |
| this / these | that / those |
| here | there |
| now | then, at that time |
| today | that day |
| tonight | that night |
| yesterday | the day before, the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day, the following day |
| last week / month | the week / month before, the previous week / month |
| next week / month | the following week / month, the next week / month |
| … ago | … before, … earlier |

'I lost **my** phone **here yesterday**.' → He said he had lost **his** phone **there the day before**.

## 3. say czy tell?

- **tell + osoba**: *She **told me** (that) she was tired.*
- **say** bez osoby albo **say to + osoba**: *She **said** (that) she was tired. She **said to me** that…*
- Błędy: ~~She said me~~, ~~She told that~~.

## 4. Pytania zależne

Szyk zdania oznajmującego (podmiot przed czasownikiem), bez *do / does / did*, bez znaku zapytania, z backshiftem.

| Typ | Wzór | Przykład |
|---|---|---|
| yes / no | asked (sb) **if / whether** + podmiot + czasownik | 'Do you like tea?' → He asked me **if I liked** tea. |
| wh- | asked (sb) **wyraz pytający** + podmiot + czasownik | 'Where does she work?' → He asked **where she worked**. |
| inne czasowniki | wanted to know, wondered | 'Is it far?' → She wondered **whether it was** far. |

Błędy: ~~asked me where did she work~~, ~~asked if did I like~~.

## 5. Polecenia, prośby, zakazy

| Typ | Wzór | Przykład |
|---|---|---|
| polecenie | **told** + osoba + **to** + bezokolicznik | 'Close the door.' → She **told me to close** the door. |
| prośba | **asked** + osoba + **to** + bezokolicznik | 'Can you help me, please?' → He **asked me to help** him. |
| zakaz | told / asked + osoba + **not to** + bezokolicznik | 'Don't run!' → She **told us not to run**. |
| ostrzeżenie, rozkaz | **warned / ordered** + osoba + (not) to | 'Don't touch it!' → He **warned me not to touch** it. |

Błędy: ~~told me to not run~~, ~~asked me that I help~~.

## 6. Czasowniki relacjonujące i ich wzorce

| Wzorzec | Czasowniki | Przykład |
|---|---|---|
| + **to** + bezokolicznik | promise, offer, refuse, agree | 'I'll pay.' → He **offered to pay**. |
| + **-ing** | suggest, recommend, admit | 'Let's walk.' → She **suggested walking**. |
| + **for + -ing** | apologise | 'Sorry for the noise.' → He **apologised for making** noise. |
| + osoba + **to** + bezokolicznik | advise, warn, remind, invite, encourage, ask, tell | 'Take a coat.' → Mum **reminded me to take** a coat. |
| + **that** + zdanie | explain, complain, say, tell + osoba | 'It's too cold.' → He **complained that** it was too cold. |

## 7. Pięć najczęstszych błędów

1. **said me** → *said to me* albo *told me*.
2. **Szyk pytania w pytaniu zależnym**: ~~asked where was he~~ → *asked where he was*.
3. **if z pytaniem wh-**: ~~asked if where he lived~~ → *asked where he lived*.
4. **Brak backshiftu po said**: ~~He said he is ill.~~ → *He said he was ill.*
5. **Zostawione okoliczniki**: ~~She said she would come tomorrow.~~ → *She said she would come the next day.*
